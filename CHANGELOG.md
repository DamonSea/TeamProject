## v1.2.0 — Controller support (2026-11-20)

![Navigating the menus with a controller](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DamonSea/Rat-Man/main/docs/media/controller-menus.gif)

- **Play with a controller.** PS5, PS4, Xbox and Switch Pro controllers work on Mac and Windows, over USB or Bluetooth. You can connect one at any time.
- **The whole game works from the couch.** Use the D-pad or left stick to move and Options / Start to pause. In every menu, the D-pad moves a white highlight, Cross / A selects and Circle / B goes back.
- **Arcade-style high score initials.** Press Up/Down to pick each letter, Cross to lock it in and Circle to fix a mistake.

  ![Entering initials with a controller](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DamonSea/Rat-Man/main/docs/media/controller-initials.gif)

- **Rumble.** The controller shakes when you lose a life, eat a cat, or when the boss roars, slams into a wall or explodes. Turn it off with the new **RUMBLE** switch in Settings.
- **Prompts match your controller.** They say Cross / Circle / Options on PlayStation controllers and A / B / Start on the others.
- **PS5 light bar** glows cheese-yellow while you play.

## v1.1.0 — Boss cinematics (2026-11-20)

![The Three-Headed Cat's entrance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DamonSea/Rat-Man/main/docs/media/boss-intro.gif)

- **A real boss entrance.** The Three-Headed Cat now arrives with a cinematic intro and a roar that shakes the screen.
- **New boss move: the Pounce.** The boss crouches, then lunges in a straight line. Dodge it and it slams into the wall and is left dizzy for a moment.

  ![The Pounce: dodge it and the boss hits the wall](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DamonSea/Rat-Man/main/docs/media/boss-pounce.gif)

- **Explosive boss defeat.** Beat the boss and it goes out with a bang.

  ![Defeating the boss](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DamonSea/Rat-Man/main/docs/media/boss-defeat.gif)

- **New death animation.** When you get caught, you spin and pop like a classic arcade game. That includes the Joe Oakes skin.

  ![Joe Oakes spins and pops](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DamonSea/Rat-Man/main/docs/media/joe-oakes-pop.gif)

## v1.0.0 — The comeback (2026-11-20)

The first downloadable version: the old team project rebuilt, fixed and filled out.

![Smooth 60fps movement](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DamonSea/Rat-Man/main/docs/media/smooth-movement.gif)

- **Mac and Windows apps.** Your friends can download and play with no Java install needed.
- **Hand-designed levels.** Each level has its own maze. Random mazes are still available as a setting.
- **Smarter cats with names and personalities:**
  - **Alpha, the Chaser**: the confident leader who comes straight at you.
  - **Explorer, the Ambusher**: the smallest and scrappiest, who cuts you off.
  - **Yoda, the Flanker**: comes at you from the side.
- **Smooth 60fps movement** for the rat and the cats.
- **Original music and sound effects**, plus a HUD for score, level and lives with some personality.
- **Glowing cheese** that's easier to spot.
- **How to Play** guide on the main menu, including an intro to each cat.
- **Settings screen** for music, sound effects and random mazes.
- **Pause menu** with Resume, Restart and Main Menu.
- **Colourful menus**, bigger outlined sprites and a bigger boss.
- **Bug fixes**, including cats and maze walls no longer ending up inside the cat cage, and the rat now turning reliably.
