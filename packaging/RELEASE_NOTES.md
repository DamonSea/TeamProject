## 🐀 Rat-Man

A Pac-Man-style maze game. You're a rat collecting cheese while three cats (**Alpha**, **Explorer** and **Yoda**) hunt you down. Grab the big cheese to turn the tables. Every 4th level, the Three-Headed Cat shows up.

**No Java needed:** everything the game needs is bundled inside.

### Download

| Your computer | Download |
|---|---|
| **Mac** (Apple Silicon: M1, M2, M3, M4…) | `Rat-Man-…-mac.dmg` |
| **Windows** 10 / 11 | `Rat-Man-…-windows.zip` |

Files are listed under **Assets** below.

### Mac: install and first launch

1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **Rat-Man** into **Applications**.
2. Open Rat-Man. macOS will say it can't verify the developer, because the game isn't signed with a paid Apple developer account. Click **Done** (or **Cancel**).
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to the Rat-Man message. Confirm with your password.
4. From then on, it opens normally.

If macOS says the app **"is damaged and can't be opened"**, open **Terminal** and run:
```
xattr -cr /Applications/Rat-Man.app
```
then open it again.

### Windows: install and first launch

1. Unzip `Rat-Man-…-windows.zip` (right-click → **Extract All**).
2. Open the **Rat-Man** folder and double-click **Rat-Man.exe**.
3. If Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info → Run anyway** (the game isn't signed with a paid certificate).

### How to play

- **Move:** arrow keys or WASD (Xbox controller works on Windows)
- **Pause:** P or Esc
- Check **HOW TO PLAY** on the main menu for the rules and the cats' strategies.
- **Settings** has toggles for music, sound effects, and random mazes.

High scores are saved on your own computer.
